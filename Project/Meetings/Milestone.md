## Agenda Items:

### 1. **Suggestion to Transition from Elasticsearch to a Vector-Based Approach**

- **Discussion Points:**
  - The current use of Elasticsearch was evaluated against the potential benefits of transitioning to a vector-based search approach.
  - Considerations included the need for improved search relevance and efficiency.
- **Action Items:**
  - Research potential vector-based search solutions (e.g., Weaviate, Pinecone).
  - Evaluate the feasibility, including cost, performance, and integration complexity.

### 2. **Approval to Utilize Large Language Models (LLM) for Answer Generation**

- **Discussion Points:**
  - The effectiveness of LLMs in generating accurate and contextually relevant answers was acknowledged.
  - Potential LLMs were discussed, with an emphasis on understanding their capabilities, limitations, and costs.
- **Action Items:**
  - Research state-of-the-art Large Language Models, which are freely available
  - Identify metrics for evaluating the success of the LLM in answer generation.

### 3. **Decision that Frontend Development Should Be the Last Phase**

- **Discussion Points:**
  - The strategy to focus on backend and API development before moving to frontend was agreed upon.
  - This approach allows for a focus on backend development
- **Action Items:**
  - Outline the project timeline with clear milestones for backend completion before frontend initiation.

### 4. **Exploration of Different Chunking and Embedding Methods**

- **Discussion Points:**
  - The need to enhance data processing and search capabilities by exploring various chunking and embedding strategies.
  - The impact of these methods on search accuracy and performance was considered.
- **Action Items:**
  - Set up a task force to research and test different chunking and embedding techniques.
  - Schedule a follow-up meeting to review findings and decide on the implementation strategy.

### 5. **Decision on Which LLM to Use**

- **Discussion Points:**
  - Various LLMs were considered based on their performance, cost, and ease of integration.
  - The importance of selecting an LLM that aligns with our specific requirements and goals was emphasized.
- **Action Items:**
  - Compile a comparative analysis of shortlisted LLMs.
  - Decide on the LLM for our project based on this analysis and schedule a meeting to finalize the decision.

## Next Steps:

- Assign team members to each action item.
- Set deadlines for research and decision-making processes.
- Schedule the next meeting to review progress and make further decisions.
