Welcome to the comprehensive guide for evaluating responses within the Medisearch project. This evaluation framework is provided to ensures objectivity and consistency, facilitating a fair and precise assessment of responses throughout the research project. It is designed to rigorously assess each answer based on three key criteria: grammatical correctness and coherence, derivation from provided texts, and the efficacy in addressing the given question. The scoring system comprises 0, 0.5, or 1 point for each criterion.

1. Grammatical Correctness and Coherence:
   - 0 Points: The response is marred by significant grammatical errors, rendering it incomprehensible and lacking structural coherence.
   - 0.5 Points: Although the answer exhibits some grammatical issues or lacks complete coherence, it remains marginally understandable.
   - 1 Point: The response is grammatically impeccable, meticulously structured, and effortlessly comprehensible.

2. Derivation from Provided Texts:
   - 0 Points: The answer demonstrates no discernible connection to the information presented in the provided texts.
   - 0.5 Points: While the response partially draws from the provided texts, it may contain inaccuracies or present incomplete information.
   - 1 Point: The answer adeptly reflects the information derived from the provided texts, exhibiting accuracy and comprehensive understanding.

3. Adequacy in Addressing the Question:
   - 0 Points: The answer fails to address the question posed, lacking any semblance of relevance.
   - 0.5 Points: While the response may touch on the question, it lacks depth or fails to cover all pertinent aspects adequately.
   - 1 Point: The answer precisely and thoroughly addresses the question, furnishing a comprehensive and well-articulated response.
